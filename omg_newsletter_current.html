Training & Compliance

Password Hygiene & MFA — Know Your First Line of Defense

Your password is the front door to everything — email, systems, data. A weak or reused password is an open invitation. Here are three things every OMG employee should have in place:

1
 Use a strong, unique password for every account

Never reuse passwords across systems. If one account is compromised, reused passwords give attackers access to everything else.
2
 Enable MFA on every account that supports it

Multi-Factor Authentication adds a second layer — even if your password is stolen, attackers still can’t get in. Microsoft 365, Salesforce, and VPN all support MFA. If yours isn’t enabled, contact IT.
3
 Never share your password — with anyone

IT will never ask for your password. If anyone requests it — by email, phone, or in person — treat it as a red flag and report it immediately.

Questions about your MFA setup or password manager? Submit a help desk ticket or reach out to the IT team directly.
