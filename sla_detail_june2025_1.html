Summary

Key Highlights

  • Combined SLA success rate improved from 83.82% in March to 91.00% in April — a gain of 7.18 percentage points.
  • Every SLA tier improved month-over-month. Low SLA showed the largest gain (+7.76pp), followed by Medium SLA (+9.64pp).
  • Ticket volume increased from 204 core tickets in March to 211 in April — growing demand with improved resolution efficiency.
  • High SLA volume grew from 42 to 48 tickets — continued monitoring and escalation procedures are recommended.
